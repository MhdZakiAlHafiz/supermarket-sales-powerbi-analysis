# Supermarket Sales & Financial Performance Dashboard

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** developed to analyze 1,000 supermarket sales transactions and evaluate sales performance, revenue, profitability, customer behavior, and payment preferences.

The dashboard transforms transaction-level sales data into interactive business insights through **Power Query, DAX measures, and data visualization**.

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing supermarket transaction data across multiple branches, cities, product lines, customer types, genders, and payment methods.

The dashboard was designed to provide a concise overview of business performance through financial KPIs, product-level analysis, customer ratings, payment distribution, and profit trends.

### Dataset

* **Records:** 1,000 transactions
* **Period:** January 2019 – March 2019
* **Branches:** A, B, C
* **Cities:** Yangon, Naypyitaw, Mandalay
* **Product Lines:** 6 categories
* **Customer Types:** Member, Normal
* **Gender:** Female, Male
* **Payment Methods:** Cash, E-wallet, Credit Card

---

## Business Objectives

The dashboard was developed to answer several business questions:

1. What are the overall revenue, net revenue, and profit generated by the supermarket?
2. Which product lines contribute the most to revenue?
3. How does profit change throughout the sales period?
4. Which product lines have the highest customer ratings?
5. How are transactions distributed across payment methods?
6. How do tax, net revenue, profit, and GPM% vary across product lines?
7. How do customer characteristics such as gender and customer type affect the analysis?

## DAX Measures

Several custom measures were created to support the financial analysis.

| Measure         | Purpose                                                   |
| --------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Revenue**     | Measures the sales revenue generated from transactions    |
| **Tax 5%**      | Calculates the 5% tax component                           |
| **Net Revenue** | Measures revenue after incorporating the 5% tax component |
| **Profit**      | Measures the profit generated from sales                  |
| **GPM %**       | Measures gross profit margin as a percentage              |
| **Quantity**    | Measures the total number of units sold                   |

These measures allow the dashboard to present both operational and financial performance in a more structured way.

## Dashboard Components

### 1. KPI Summary

The dashboard provides an overview of the main business metrics:

* **1,000** total transactions
* **5,510** total units sold
* **307.59K** Revenue
* **322.97K** Net Revenue
* **15.38K** Profit

These KPIs provide a quick snapshot of overall supermarket performance.

### 2. Profit Trend

A line chart is used to analyze profit movement throughout the January–March 2019 period.

This allows users to identify fluctuations in daily profit performance.

### 3. Revenue by Product Line

The dashboard compares revenue contributions across the six product lines.

**Food and beverages** generates the highest revenue contribution at approximately **56.14K**.

### 4. Customer Rating by Product Line

Average customer ratings are compared across product lines.

The ratings range from approximately **6.84 to 7.11**, allowing product categories with relatively higher and lower customer satisfaction to be identified.

### 5. Payment Method Distribution

A donut chart presents the distribution of transactions across:

* E-wallet
* Cash
* Credit Card

The distribution is relatively balanced, with E-wallet and Cash representing the largest shares.

### 6. Product Financial Matrix

A matrix provides product-level financial information including:

* Tax 5%
* Net Revenue
* Profit
* GPM %

This enables users to examine financial performance at a more detailed product-line level.

### 7. Interactive Filters

The dashboard provides interactive filtering options including:

* Gender
* Customer Type
* Rating

These filters allow users to explore specific customer segments and analyze how the selected segment affects dashboard results.

## Key Insights

### Product Performance

**Food and beverages** is the leading product line in terms of revenue, generating approximately **56.14K** in revenue.

### Customer Satisfaction

Average ratings across product lines are relatively consistent, ranging from approximately **6.84 to 7.11**.

Food and beverages has the highest average rating among the product lines shown in the dashboard.

### Payment Behavior

Payment usage is relatively evenly distributed between E-wallet, Cash, and Credit Card, indicating that customers use multiple payment channels rather than relying heavily on a single method.

### Financial Performance

The dashboard reports approximately:

* **307.59K Revenue**
* **322.97K Net Revenue**
* **15.38K Profit**

These metrics provide an overview of the supermarket's financial performance during the analyzed period.

## Business Recommendations

Based on the dashboard analysis:

1. **Strengthen high-performing product categories**
   Food and beverages demonstrates strong revenue performance and customer ratings. The category can be considered for promotional campaigns and product bundling strategies.

2. **Monitor product-level profitability**
   The financial matrix can be used to identify differences in profit and GPM% across product lines and support future pricing or promotional decisions.

3. **Maintain multiple payment options**
   Since payment usage is relatively balanced, maintaining Cash, E-wallet, and Credit Card options can help accommodate different customer preferences.

4. **Monitor customer satisfaction**
   Product lines with comparatively lower ratings can be investigated further to identify potential opportunities for improving product or service quality.
